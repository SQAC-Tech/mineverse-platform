# MINEVERSE — Event Summary

**Mineverse** is a Minecraft-themed coding event where teams of 2–3 players progress through four biomes by solving coding, debugging, and logic challenges, earning resources, crafting pickaxes, and battling guardians. The event runs over two days and ends with a Final Boss battle in The End.

## Concept
Teams solve coding problems to earn resources (Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamonds, Emeralds), craft progressively better gear, catch reward windows, and trade at the Marketplace. The goal: survive all biomes, craft the Diamond Pickaxe, and defeat the Ender Dragon first to become MINEVERSE champions.

## Structure at a Glance

| Stage | Biome / Round | Focus | Key Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Forest & Grasslands | Crossword, aptitude, output-prediction questions; optional Forest Guardian | Craft Wooden Pickaxe |
| Round 2 | Cave Biome | Aptitude, debugging, code completion; Skeleton Archer. Offline games run alongside, off the platform | Craft Stone Pickaxe |
| Round 3 | Mountain Biome | Debugging, coding, physical games; **Elimination round** — Blaze Guardian, PvP | Craft Iron Armor, win PvP (Nether Core), top 50% qualify (Day 2) |
| Pre-Final Round | Nether Portal | Team games in the room, off the platform. The platform handles only the portal repair | Repair Nether Portal |
| Final Round | The End | Leetcode-style coding, logic puzzles, debugging | Craft Diamond Pickaxe, Final Boss Challenge |

## Day-wise Flow
- **Day 1:** Registration → Opening Ceremony → Gameplay Briefing → Rounds 1–3 (Forest, Cave, Mountain biomes) → PvP Qualification & Leaderboard → Fun Activities & Day 1 Closing.
- **Day 2:** Day 1 Recap → Pre-Final Round → Final Round → Final Result Compilation → Prize Distribution & Closing Ceremony.

## Winning Condition
The first team to defeat the Final Boss (after crafting the Diamond Pickaxe) is crowned the **Champion of MINEVERSE**.

## Core Elements
- **Resources:** Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Obsidian
- **Guardians:** Forest Guardian, Skeleton Archer, Blaze Guardian, Final Boss
- **World Events:** Reward windows only — Heavy Rain, Fertile Marsh, Gold Rush and Chorus Fruit Blessing each double one resource for five minutes. Nothing can take resources away.
- **Choice Events:** Ancient Shrine (R2), Piglin Merchant (R3), End Merchant (R5)
- **Marketplace:** A Villager Merchant trades Emeralds for hints, resource bundles, and power-ups throughout the event
- **Offline games:** Played in the room. The platform records nothing; organizers credit what a team earned through the admin Grant Resources screen.
